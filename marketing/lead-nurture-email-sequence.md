**Body:**

> Here is your download link for the guide:

> 
> Before you dive into the 50 phrases, here is the #1 mistake I see nomads make across CDMX, Medellín, and Buenos Aires:
> 
> **Translating "Can I have..." word for word.**
> 
> In English, saying *"Can I have a coffee?"* is the gold standard of politeness.  
> But in Spanish, blurting out *"¿Puedo tener un café?"* literally sounds like:  
> *"Am I physically capable of possessing a coffee?"*
> 
> The barista immediately knows you’re translating in your head—and switches straight to English.
> 
> Depending on where you are right now, use this instead:
> * 🇲🇽 **CDMX:** *"¿Me da un café, porfa?"*
> * 🇨🇴 **Medellín:** *"¿Me regala un café, porfa?"*
> * 🇪🇸 **Madrid:** *"Ponme un café, porfa."*
> * 🇦🇷 **Buenos Aires:** *"¿Te pido un café?"*
