The Street Phrasebook

Everyday Latin American Spanish in Action

Here are the exact phrases we train in our 1-on-1 sessions. Click through the common nomad situations below to see how locals communicate.

Situation:
☕ Cafe & Workspace
"¡Buenas! ¿Cómo le va? ¿Me regala la clave del wifi y una mesita cerca al enchufe, porfa? Vengo a trabajar un ratico."
In plain English:

"Hello! How is it going? Could you give me the wifi password and a table near the socket, please? I have come to work for a little bit."

Street breakdown:

In Colombia and across much of Latin America, opening with "¿Me regala...?" is the ultimate polite, warm greeting. Saying "un ratico" adds friendly natural warmth that staff love.
