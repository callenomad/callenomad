How to Negotiate Rent in Medellín: Stop Paying the Gringo Tax

Arriving in Medellín and renting an apartment through Facebook groups or direct landlords often triggers an automatic 30-50% surcharge if you speak textbook tourist Spanish. Landlords immediately identify newcomers. When you use authentic Paisa phrasing and business etiquette, you signal local experience and command fair local pricing.

🗣️ Textbook vs. Street Spanish

Here is what standard apps teach you versus what locals actually say and respect on the street:

Situation / Intent Textbook Spanish ❌ Calle Spanish ✅ Cultural Nuance
Asking for the best price / discount ¿Tiene algún descuento? ¿Cuánto es lo menos por el mes? Direct yet culturally expected in Colombian market negotiation. Textbook Spanish sounds rigid and timid; Paisas respect a direct, polite counter.
Confirming an agreed price or deal Estoy de acuerdo con el precio. Listo parcero, me sirve. Cerremos de una. Demonstrates decisive Colombian casual confidence. Landlords prefer hassle-free tenants who confirm quickly.
Asking if utilities (services) are included ¿Los servicios públicos están incluidos? ¿Eso viene con servicios full o toca pagar aparte? Natural street phrasing distinguishing all-inclusive rent from variable monthly bills (EPM).
Inquiring about lease duration flexibility ¿Puedo alquilar por tres meses? La idea es quedarme tres meses firmes. ¿Cómo me lo deja por ese tiempo? Frames length of stay as negotiation leverage rather than asking for permission.

📱 Ready-to-Send WhatsApp Scripts

Copy, paste, and customize these battle-tested messages directly in WhatsApp:

Initial Inquiry on Facebook Marketplace / Direct Owner
¡Buenas tardes! Vi la publicación del apartamento en Laureles. Me interesa bastante. La idea es quedarme tres meses firmes desde el próximo lunes. ¿Cuánto es lo menos que me lo dejaría con servicios incluidos? Quedo atento, muchas gracias.
Translation: “Good afternoon! I saw your post for the apartment in Laureles. I am very interested. My plan is to stay three solid months starting next Monday. What is your bottom price with utilities included? Looking forward to hearing from you, thank you!”
Confirming Viewing Appointment
¡Hola! Listo, me queda perfecto a las 4:00 PM hoy. ¿Me regala la dirección exacta y el número del apto? Allá llego puntual.
Translation: “Hello! Done, 4:00 PM today works perfectly for me. Could you share the exact address and apartment number? I will be there punctually.”
